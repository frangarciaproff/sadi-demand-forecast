How it works

  • Model: a linear response to heating and cooling degree days plus gradient-boosted trees on the rest (calendar, recent days), on the change against the median of the same weekday in the previous four weeks.
  • Inputs as known at the issue time: the operator's daily reports already published (three or more days old), the 48-hour-lead GFS temperature forecast at 15 demand centres, and the holiday calendar.
  • Retrained every month on the values the operator has published by then.
  • The operator's programme is published about 11 hours later and uses real-time operating data, so the comparison favours it.
